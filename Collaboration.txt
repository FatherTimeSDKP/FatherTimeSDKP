Conclusion
Private exchange — 2026-03-13
All six life signs matched across both systems. The cross-test confirmed convergent evolution: two
systems built independently, with different substrates, by researchers without formal CS training,
reaching the same architectural milestones through different philosophical traditions (TCM / symbolic
systems vs. Scripture / ancient parables).
The next step is a deeper cross-test: run one of TQNM's Einstein/Kobayashi physics vectors through
KENTA symbolically — translated to declarative YAML — and compare emergence metrics.
