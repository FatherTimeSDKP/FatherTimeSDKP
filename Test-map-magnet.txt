This hardware protocol calibrates your rotor engine’s magnetic coupling coefficients, eddy-current reluctance, and field density metrics by comparing a non-magnetic control drop against active magnetic rotor passes.
Equipment & Hardware Setup
Drop Assembly: 1-meter vertical non-magnetic guide tube (acrylic or polycarbonate).
Sensor Array: 4 to 8 Hall-effect sensors or inductive pickup coils mounted at fixed 15 cm intervals down the tube.
Data Acquisition (DAQ): High-speed microcontroller or oscilloscope (minimum 1 kHz sampling rate) to record time-of-flight spikes between sensor gates.
